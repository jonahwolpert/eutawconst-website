Why Lake Marion Calls Us

We Know This Water Because We Live On It

Our founder Tommy Wolpert started in marine construction in the 1980s. He started his own business in the 2000s, and formed Eutaw Construction LLC in 2020 to continue the work alongside his son Jonah. That's over four decades of hands-on experience on the Santee Cooper system.

We are not an out-of-town contractor who found Lake Marion on a map. We are based in Eutawville — twenty minutes from most project sites on the lake. We know the bottom conditions in your cove, the permitting process, and how Santee Cooper's water level management affects the way your dock needs to be built.

When the job is finished, you will not wonder whether it was done right. You will know.

Where We Work

Lake Marion, Lake Moultrie & the Santee Cooper System
