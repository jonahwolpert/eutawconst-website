Custom Dock Construction

Docks Engineered for Your Waterfront

We design and build custom docks for residential lakefront properties across Lake Marion and Santee Cooper. Whether you need a simple single-slip dock or a multi-slip structure with a covered boat slip, we engineer each dock to match your property's specific conditions — water depth, bottom composition, exposure to wave and wake action, and how you plan to use the water.

Materials include pressure-treated lumber, composite decking, and galvanized or aluminum hardware rated for freshwater. Every dock is built to comply with South Carolina DHEC and Santee Cooper permitting requirements, and we handle permit coordination as part of your project.

What's included: site assessment, engineering layout, permit coordination, full installation, and final walkthrough.

Floating Dock Systems

The Right Choice for Managed Water Levels

Santee Cooper manages Lake Marion's water level year-round, and seasonal fluctuation can reach several feet. Unlike a fixed dock, a floating system rises and falls with the water — keeping your boat access consistent no matter where the lake sits.

We install residential floating dock systems and heavy-duty commercial platforms for marinas, fishing operations, and utility access points on the Santee Cooper reservoir.

Boat Lift Installation

Protect Your Boat From the Water When You're Not On It

A properly installed boat lift extends your boat's life and prevents hull damage from prolonged water contact. We install hydraulic and electric boat lifts rated for every vessel type — small fishing boats, pontoons, tritoons, cabin cruisers.

Lifts can be mounted to your existing dock or installed on independent pilings, depending on your slip configuration. We source the appropriate lift system for your boat's weight and beam, and handle all structural mounting and electrical coordination.

Seawalls & Shoreline Protection

Stop the Erosion Before It Takes Your Yard

Lakefront erosion on Lake Marion can strip significant shoreline in a few seasons. A properly designed seawall stops wave action, boat wakes, and the effects of fluctuating water levels before they undermine your foundation, yard, or dock.

We construct new seawalls in concrete, vinyl sheet pile, and riprap configurations depending on site conditions, budget, and goals. We also assess and repair failing seawalls — repointing, cap replacement, anchor tie-back systems, and full reconstruction where needed.

Get a Seawall Assessment
Boat Ramp Construction

Safe, Graded Launches for Residential & Commercial Use

A properly graded concrete boat ramp makes launching and retrieving simple and safe — for years. We construct residential and commercial ramps on Lake Marion and Santee Cooper waterways, including sub-base grading, concrete forming and pour, and integration with adjacent dock structures where needed.

Repair & Maintenance

Catch Small Problems Before They Become Big Ones

An aging dock gets ahead of you fast. We provide inspection, repair, and seasonal maintenance for residential dock owners across Lake Marion — piling inspection and replacement, decking replacement, railing repair, hardware replacement, and flotation service for floating docks.

Annual pre-season inspections catch small failures before they become full rebuilds. Contact us in late winter to schedule before the boating rush begins.
