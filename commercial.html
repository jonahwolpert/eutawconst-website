Why Local Matters

A Contractor That Actually Lives on Santee Cooper

Too many Santee Cooper projects end up in the hands of contractors who travel hours to the site, subcontract the actual work, and leave when the final invoice is paid. The results speak for themselves — seawalls that fail the next winter, pilings set in the wrong substrate, repairs that should not have been repairs in the first place.

Eutaw Construction is based in Eutawville, SC, on the shore of Lake Marion. The principals are on-site for every project. Our crew and equipment are already staged within the reservoir system. When something needs to be done twice because a storm pushed back the schedule, we are still here — not packing up for the drive home.

We want to be the local option that Santee Cooper, municipalities, and commercial owners reach for first.

Commercial Capabilities

Infrastructure, Utility & Heavy Marine Work

We bid subcontract and prime on projects across the Santee Cooper system.

Utility & Municipal Marine Work

Shoreline infrastructure, bank stabilization, erosion remediation, pipeline and outfall protection, and riprap placement for utility right-of-way maintenance.

Commercial Docks & Piers

Marina piers, restaurant and resort docks, community boat slips, and HOA-managed waterfront structures. Engineered for commercial load ratings and public use.

Seawall & Shoreline Systems

Large-scale seawall construction and repair in concrete, steel and vinyl sheet pile, and engineered riprap. Shoreline protection for utility parcels and commercial properties.

Boat Ramps & Public Access

Concrete boat ramps, courtesy docks, and public-access waterfront installations with load-rated surfaces and gradients engineered for Santee Cooper water levels.

Piling, Driving & Replacement

Timber, concrete, and steel pile driving and replacement for commercial structures. Mobile equipment staged locally.

Emergency & Storm Response

Post-storm marine structure inspection, debris removal, and emergency repair. Same-week dispatch for projects within the reservoir system.
